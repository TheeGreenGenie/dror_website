About Us

Who we are

Divine Rock of Refuge is an outreach organization that partners with churches around the world — especially in Nigeria and the Philadelphia area — to run clothing drives, shoe drives, and youth sporting tournaments.

Our Mission

Why we exist

Our mission is to be a refuge in a practical sense: to make sure that a child has shoes that fit, that a family has warm clothing before winter, and that young people have somewhere to belong.

We do this by working through churches rather than around them. Our partner congregations in Nigeria and the greater Philadelphia area already know their communities — who is struggling, which families were missed, which kids need a team. We supply the drives, the donations, and the tournaments; they make sure it reaches the right doors.

The work is simple and it is repeatable. Collect, sort, distribute. Organize, coach, celebrate. Then do it again in the next community that asks.

Where We Work

Two homes, one network

We partner with churches wherever the need and the relationship exist, with our deepest roots in these two places.

Nigeria

Working with local congregations to distribute clothing and footwear to families and schoolchildren, and to organize tournaments that bring whole neighborhoods out to watch.

Philadelphia Area

Collection points, sorting days, and winter coat drives run out of partner churches across the city and surrounding communities — plus youth sports programming for local teams.
