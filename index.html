Our Work

Churches on the ground, everywhere we go

Divine Rock of Refuge partners with local churches around the world — with a deep focus in Nigeria and the greater Philadelphia area — to meet real, practical needs in their communities.

Those churches already know their neighborhoods. We bring the drives, the donations, and the tournaments; they bring the relationships that make sure everything lands where it is needed most.

Our Programs

Three ways we show up

Every program runs through a partner church, so donations reach families directly and young people are supported by people who know them.

Clothing Drives

We collect, sort, and distribute clothing through partner churches — coats and warm layers for Philadelphia winters, everyday essentials in Nigeria.

Shoe Drives

A dedicated push for new and gently used footwear. A solid pair of shoes means a child can walk to school, play safely, and keep up.

Youth Sports Tournaments

We organize tournaments that give young people something to train for, a team to belong to, and mentors in the stands.
